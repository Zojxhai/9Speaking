Official IELTS Speaking Band Descriptors

Scoring criteria for Academic and General Training tests (Bands 5 – 9)

Band 9 — Expert User Fully Operational Command
Fluency & Coherence

Fluent with only very occasional repetition or self-correction. Any hesitation is content-related.

Lexical Resource

Total flexibility and precise use in all contexts. Sustained use of accurate idiomatic language.

Grammatical Range & Accuracy

Structures are precise and accurate at all times, apart from characteristic native-speaker slips.

Pronunciation

Full range of phonological features to convey subtle meaning. Flexible connected speech throughout.

Band 8 — Very Good User Occasional Inaccuracies
Fluency & Coherence

Fluent with only occasional repetition. Hesitation mostly content-related. Coherent and relevant topic extension.

Lexical Resource

Wide resource flexibly used. Skilful use of less common/idiomatic items despite rare collocation slips.

Grammatical Range & Accuracy

Wide range of structures flexibly used. The majority of sentences are error-free.

Pronunciation

Wide range of phonological features. Sustains appropriate rhythm and intonation. Easily understood.

Band 7 — Good User Operational Command
Fluency & Coherence

Produces long turns without noticeable effort. Some mid-sentence hesitations. Flexible use of discourse markers.

Lexical Resource

Resource flexibly used across diverse topics. Uses less common items with awareness of style and collocation.

Grammatical Range & Accuracy

Mix of simple and complex structures. Frequent error-free sentences, though basic errors persist.

Pronunciation

Generally clear intonation and rhythm despite occasional lapses.
